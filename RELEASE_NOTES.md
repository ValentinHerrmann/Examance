## What's Changed
* Make the four layout templates your own: save your current layout (paper, extra pages, info text and more) into a template, rename or reset it, with new everyday defaults (Examance booklet, Examance anonymous, anonymous on paper, classic paper test) (#279) by @ValentinHerrmann in https://github.com/ValentinHerrmann/Examance/pull/336
* Downloaded files are named by exam date, subject, class, exam type and number (e.g. 2026-11-05_Informatik_10a_Kurzarbeit_1_Loesung.pdf), so they sort by date (#337) by @ValentinHerrmann in https://github.com/ValentinHerrmann/Examance/pull/338
* Objections stay open until you have answered every exercise the student objected to, shown as "Partly answered" until then (#280) by @ValentinHerrmann in https://github.com/ValentinHerrmann/Examance/pull/342
