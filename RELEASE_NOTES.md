## What's Changed
* Exams on several sheets: a small page code on every page, double-sided or A3 booklet printing (arranged by Examance or your printer) with a wide cut band at the fold, filler pages for side calculations, sticker spots per exam, sheet, A3 sheet or A4 leaf, print options as clear tiles with one "Create print files" button and a preview of exactly what gets printed, a self-saving Layout tab, and scans sorted by page with warnings for missing pages (#220) by @ValentinHerrmann in https://github.com/ValentinHerrmann/Examance/pull/230
* Extra paper: squared, lined or empty writing space with extra pages, copies filled up to whole sheets, a printable extra-sheet stack and late extra sheets added to the right scan; every exam setting now saves itself; booklets that come out centred, with a printed fold line, a shift setting and a test sheet; more scissors on the cut lines; uncut booklet sheets scanned from any scanner; an option to print exams without QR codes and markers when grading outside Examance; paper grading keeps the scores of every listed student; setting choices shown as boxed tiles (#228) by @ValentinHerrmann in https://github.com/ValentinHerrmann/Examance/pull/231
