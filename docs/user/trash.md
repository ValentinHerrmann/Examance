# Trash: undo a delete

Whatever you delete in Examance by mistake can come back for **7 days**. Open **Trash** in the account menu, next to Settings.

## What goes to the trash

- Exams, with their students, scanned sheets and scores.
- A single student, a single scanned sheet, or all sheets of an exam ("delete all submissions").
- Scores and corrections you clear: the scores of a sheet, its grading, or only its annotations.
- Exercises from your library and the files attached to them (images, PDFs, data files).
- Exercises and groups of multiple-choice questions you remove from an exam while editing it.
- Your logo and an exam's own logo when you remove or replace them.
- Proposals for a shared exercise that you reject or withdraw.

Everything you delete in one go (for example a student together with their scans) shows up as one entry and is restored or removed together.

Trash entries stay as protected as the original: names, scans and corrections remain encrypted, and so does the trash copy.

## Restore or delete permanently

For each entry you can:

- **Restore** it. Examance puts back what is missing. It never overwrites anything you have now: if you created the same thing again in the meantime, the current version stays. When part of an entry cannot come back, the page tells you what and why.
- **Delete permanently** to erase it at once, including the scanned sheets. Without this, an entry disappears by itself after 7 days.

## What cannot always come back

- **Parts whose parent is gone.** A scanned sheet cannot return into an exam that no longer exists. If the exam is still in the trash, restore it first: it brings its sheets with it.
- **A grading you have changed since.** If you cleared a sheet's grading and then graded it again, restoring does not overwrite the new grading.
- **An exercise version that is no longer the newest.** If another version of the exercise has become current since, the restored version comes back as an older version.
- **Results from before a pseudonym update.** After Examance re-keys the pseudonyms of an exam, older entries of its students no longer match anyone and are skipped.
- **Results when your account may not store them on the server.** Restoring students, sheets and scores needs the "results on the server" permission of your account, the same as saving results.
- **Anything older than 7 days.** It is erased for good.

## Hybrid mode

If your results are stored in the browser ("Hybrid"), the trash for those results is in the same browser too. It appears on the same page, but you can restore it only in that browser and only while your account is still in hybrid mode. Back up your results with a `.examance` archive as usual; the trash is no replacement for a backup.

## Erasing data on request

The erasure of a student's data in **Settings** (the right to erasure under GDPR) is immediate and does **not** go through the trash. It cannot be undone. Deleting your account removes your trash as well.

## Need something older?

Ask your administrator. Backups can bring back an entry from older than 7 days, as long as a backup from the time still exists. The administrator copies it back into your trash and you restore it from there.
